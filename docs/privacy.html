SpeakAlert Privacy Policy

We collect nothing. Your reminders stay yours.

SpeakAlert is an Android voice reminder app made by GhostGram Labs. The app has no internet access, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never receive, see, store, sell or share your reminders, recordings or any other personal data.

Effective date: 22 November 2026

1. Who we are

SpeakAlert (package com.ghostgramlabs.speakalert) is published on Google Play by GhostGram Labs ("we", "us"). This policy covers the SpeakAlert Android app and its Wear OS notifications. Questions: ghostgramlabs@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect: none

SpeakAlert does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, uses no advertising ID, and has no account or sign-in. We do not collect, receive, sell, rent, share or profile any personal data.

3. What the app keeps on your device

To work, the app stores the following only in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your reminders: text, times, repeat rules and status (done, missed, snoozed)
  • Voice recordings you make for reminders
  • A reference to any audio file you pick as a reminder sound (the file itself stays where it is)
  • Your settings, such as language, theme, quiet hours and snooze length

Other apps cannot read this storage; Android's app sandbox and your device's encryption protect it. We have no access to it.

4. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Microphone: only while you record a voice reminder. Nothing is recorded in the background.
  • Notifications, full-screen alerts, exact alarms, vibration, wake lock: to ring reminders on time, including on the lock screen.
  • Run at startup: to reschedule reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): only if you allow reminders to ring during Do Not Disturb.
  • Battery optimisation exemption (optional): so the phone does not delay reminders.
  • Bluetooth connect: to play reminders privately through Bluetooth headphones or hearing aids.
  • Media playback service: to play a reminder until you respond.
  • Storage (Android 12 and older only): to read an audio file you choose and to save or open backup files.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's Settings.

5. Features that involve other software

  • Text-to-speech: typed reminders are read aloud by the speech engine installed on your phone (for example Google's or Samsung's). That engine is chosen by you or your phone maker and works under its own privacy policy.
  • Android backup: if backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may include SpeakAlert's reminders and settings (and, on Android 11 and older, recordings) in your own Google account backup, so they return when you set up a new phone. This backup is created, encrypted and stored by Google for you; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in Settings > Google > Backup.
  • Backup files you export: when you use Back up in the app, the file is saved where you choose (for example your phone or a cloud drive). What happens to it there is up to you and that service.
  • Wear OS watches: reminder notifications reach a paired watch through Android and Wear OS notification sharing, as for any other app. The app only checks whether a watch is connected.
  • Google Play: installs, updates and the optional in-app rating prompt are handled by Google Play. Google may provide us with aggregate statistics and anonymised crash and performance reports under Google's privacy policy. These contain no reminder content.

6. How long data is kept and how to delete it

Everything stays on your phone (and in any backup you or Android make for you) until you remove it. Delete a reminder in the app to erase it and its recording. To erase everything, clear the app's storage in Settings > Apps > SpeakAlert or uninstall the app. To remove an Android backup, delete it in your Google account's backup settings. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your data and to object to its use. All your SpeakAlert data is on your own device (or in your own backups) and fully under your control, so you can exercise these rights directly in the app. We do not sell or share personal information, and we make no automated decisions about you. If you have a question or complaint, email us; you may also contact your local data protection authority.

8. Children

SpeakAlert is a general reminder app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

9. Security

With no servers and no data transfer, there is nothing for us to lose in a breach. Keep your phone locked and up to date to protect what is stored on it. Report security issues to the email below.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released, and describe the change in the app's release notes. We will not start collecting personal data without telling you first.
