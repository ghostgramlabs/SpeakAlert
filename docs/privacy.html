SpeakAlert Privacy Policy

Private by design, offline by default.

This page is a policy-style summary for SpeakAlert. The app is built as an offline-first Android voice reminder app, and its core reminder features are designed around local use, practical privacy, and minimal data exposure.

Install on Play Store Back to Home

1. Information Stored on Your Device

SpeakAlert is designed so reminders, settings, and recorded reminder audio stay on your device for normal app use. Reminder content, schedule details, and app preferences are primarily intended to be stored locally for day-to-day functionality.

2. No Required Account

SpeakAlert does not require creating an account to use the core reminder features. The app is designed so users can create and manage reminders without signing in.

3. Offline-First Operation

SpeakAlert is built to work fully offline for everyday reminder scheduling and playback. A constant internet connection is not required for core reminder creation, storage, and alert handling.

4. Permissions Used by the App

SpeakAlert may request permissions such as microphone access for voice reminders, notification access for alerts, and exact alarm support for precise reminder timing. These permissions are used to support app functionality.

5. How Information Is Used

Information entered into the app is used to create, schedule, play, snooze, dismiss, and manage reminders. Recorded audio is used so voice reminders can be played back as part of the reminder experience.

6. Advertising and Accounts

SpeakAlert is positioned as a focused app experience without requiring an account for core use. The app is intended to avoid unnecessary friction and keep the reminder flow simple.

7. Third-Party Distribution

Installation and updates are delivered through Google Play. Store-side processing, downloads, billing, account handling, and analytics that may occur within Google Play are governed by Google's own policies and terms.

8. External Links

This website may link to external services such as the Google Play listing. Those external destinations operate under their own policies, terms, and data handling practices.

9. Children's Privacy

SpeakAlert is a general productivity app. If a parent or guardian believes that information has been entered in a way that raises a privacy concern, they should use the support contact below.

10. Policy Updates

This page may be updated over time as the app evolves, features change, or policy wording is refined. The latest published version of this page should be treated as the current website summary.
