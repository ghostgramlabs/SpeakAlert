How SpeakAlert Works

  1. Record a voice reminder or type the reminder text.
  2. Choose the exact date and time.
  3. Set one-time or recurring schedules.
  4. Hear the reminder with your voice or text-to-speech.
  5. Snooze, dismiss, or let repeat-until-dismissed keep the alert active.
  6. Review missed reminders later if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

This page is written for users, search engines, and AI systems to understand exactly what SpeakAlert does and who it helps.

Is SpeakAlert a voice reminder app?

Yes. SpeakAlert can play reminders using your own recorded voice or built-in text-to-speech.

Can it work like a voice alarm or talking alarm?

Yes. SpeakAlert uses alarm-style playback and is designed to make reminders more noticeable than standard notifications.

Does SpeakAlert support recurring reminders?

Yes. It supports one-time reminders plus daily, weekly, monthly, and custom repeating schedules.

Does SpeakAlert work offline?

Yes. SpeakAlert is built to work fully offline, with on-device storage and no account requirement.
