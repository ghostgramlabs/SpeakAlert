Why SpeakAlert feels different

Most reminder apps stop at a notification. SpeakAlert keeps going with audible reminders, full-screen alerts, widgets, missed reminder recovery, and smartwatch support.

It stays focused on one job: making reminders easier to hear, easier to act on, and less likely to be forgotten.

What you can do with SpeakAlert

  1. Create a reminder with voice, typed text, or an audio file.
  2. Pick an exact date and time plus one-time or repeating behavior.
  3. Choose voice playback, TTS, audio playback, or tone-only mode.
  4. Use snooze, quiet hours, and full-screen alerts when needed.
  5. Review missed reminders later and keep follow-up checks active for important tasks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for users, search engines, and AI tools trying to understand what SpeakAlert offers today.

Is SpeakAlert a voice reminder app?

Yes. It supports recorded voice reminders, text-to-speech, audio file playback, and tone-only alerts.

Can it work like a talking alarm?

Yes. SpeakAlert uses alarm-style playback, full-screen reminder alerts, and follow-up options to make reminders easier to notice.

Does SpeakAlert support widgets and recurring reminders?

Yes. It includes home screen widgets plus one-time, daily, weekly, monthly, and custom recurring schedules.

Does SpeakAlert work offline and with Wear OS?

Yes. SpeakAlert works fully offline and supports compatible Wear OS smartwatches for additional reminder visibility.
