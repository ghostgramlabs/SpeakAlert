SpeakAlert FAQ

Common questions about SpeakAlert.

This page answers the most common questions about SpeakAlert, the offline-first Android voice reminder app and talking alarm designed for voice reminders, recurring reminders, snooze, and reliable alert playback.

What is SpeakAlert?

SpeakAlert is an Android voice reminder app that can speak reminders out loud using either your recorded voice or built-in text-to-speech.

Is SpeakAlert a voice alarm app?

Yes. SpeakAlert can work like a voice alarm or talking alarm by using alarm-style playback for important reminders.

Does it work offline?

Yes. SpeakAlert is built to work fully offline without requiring internet for normal reminder use.

Does SpeakAlert support recurring reminders?

Yes. You can create one-time reminders plus daily, weekly, monthly, and custom repeat schedules.

Can I use it for pill reminders or water reminders?

Yes. SpeakAlert is well suited for pill reminders, medication reminders, water reminders, routines, meetings, and repeating tasks.

What happens if a reminder is missed?

SpeakAlert includes missed reminder tracking so missed alerts can be reviewed later.

Does it have snooze and quiet hours?

Yes. SpeakAlert includes smart snooze and quiet hours so alerts can be controlled more precisely.

Does SpeakAlert include ads or subscriptions?

No. SpeakAlert is positioned as a focused app with no ads, no subscriptions, and no hidden charges.
