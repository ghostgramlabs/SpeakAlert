## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
SpeakAlert is a free voice reminder app and talking alarm that plays reminders out loud — in your own recorded voice, with text-to-speech (TTS), or with any audio file. Unlike reminder apps with silent notifications, SpeakAlert makes sure you actually hear your reminders, even when your phone is in your pocket or across the room.

It works fully offline, needs no account, and has no ads — a simple, reliable talking reminder and voice alarm in one app.

🎙️ REMINDERS THAT TALK
• Record a voice reminder in seconds and hear it back in your own voice at the right time
• Type a reminder and let text-to-speech read it aloud
• Use any music or audio file as a custom reminder sound
• Multilingual speech: a Hindi reminder is spoken in Hindi, a Spanish one in Spanish — automatically

⏰ POWERFUL SCHEDULING
• One-time or repeating reminders: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or custom intervals (every 30 minutes, every 2 hours, every 3 days…)
• End repeats never, on a date, or after a set number of times
• Hourly reminders for water, medication, or posture breaks
• Exact alarm scheduling — fires at precisely the right minute

✅ REMINDS YOU UNTIL IT'S DONE
• Follow-up check asks "Did you complete this?" and keeps re-alerting until you mark it Done
• Keep the reminder pinned on screen until you tap Done or Snooze
• Full-screen lock screen alerts with big Done / Silence / Snooze buttons
• Missed reminder inbox and recovery — nothing slips away silently

🔔 RELIABLE ALERTS
• Tone-only mode: a loud alarm tone for maximum reliability, with your own alert sound
• Alert even during Do Not Disturb (optional DND bypass)
• Reminders survive phone restarts — everything is rescheduled automatically
• Built-in battery optimization guide for Xiaomi, POCO, and other restrictive phones

🎧 HEAR IT YOUR WAY
• Private playback through your earpiece, Bluetooth headphones, or hearing aids — reminders only you can hear
• Automatic playback with optional looping until you respond
• Custom snooze length and quiet hours for peaceful nights

⌚ WEAR OS SMARTWATCH SUPPORT
• Get talking reminder alerts on your Wear OS watch
• Act on reminders right from your wrist

🧩 WIDGETS & ORGANIZATION
• Quick Reminder widget adds a reminder straight from your home screen
• Scrollable Upcoming widget shows your next and missed reminders
• Today / Upcoming / Missed / Done tabs, one-tap duplicate

💾 BACKUP & LANGUAGES
• Back up and restore all reminders — voice recordings included — even to a new phone
• App in English, Spanish, Hindi & Arabic with RTL support, light & dark themes

🔒 PRIVATE, OFFLINE & FREE
• 100% offline — no account, no internet required
• All reminders and recordings stay on your device
• No ads. No subscriptions. Completely free.

📌 PERFECT FOR
• Medication and pill reminders — for you or elderly parents
• Daily tasks, to-dos, meetings, and appointment alerts
• Drink water, workout, cooking, and break reminders
• Bills, errands, pickups, and birthdays
• Seniors and anyone who prefers hearing reminders instead of reading silent notifications

Most reminder apps rely on silent notifications. SpeakAlert is a talking reminder and voice alarm that uses sound and speech, so you never miss what matters. Give your reminders a voice — download SpeakAlert free today.
```
